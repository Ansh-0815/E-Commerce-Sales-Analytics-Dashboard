# Business Insights

## Executive Overview

### Revenue Performance

* The business generated approximately **R$13.59M** in total revenue from nearly **99K orders**.
* Revenue showed a strong upward trend throughout most of the analyzed period, indicating consistent business growth.
* The final period experienced a noticeable decline, which may be due to incomplete data collection or seasonal fluctuations.

### Customer Activity

* The platform served over **96K unique customers**, demonstrating a broad customer base.
* Average Order Value (AOV) remained around **R$136**, indicating stable customer spending behavior.

### Delivery Performance

* More than **97% of orders were successfully delivered**, reflecting strong operational efficiency and customer fulfillment performance.
* Failed, canceled, and unavailable orders represented only a small fraction of total transactions.

### Payment Trends

* Credit cards were the most preferred payment method, accounting for the majority of transactions.
* Alternative payment methods such as vouchers and debit cards contributed a comparatively smaller share.

---

## Customer Analysis

### Geographic Distribution

* São Paulo (SP) generated the highest revenue and order volume among all states.
* Revenue contribution is heavily concentrated in a small number of regions, suggesting market dependence on key states.

### Customer Concentration

* Customer distribution closely aligns with revenue performance, indicating that regions with more customers also generate higher sales.
* A limited number of states contribute a significant portion of overall business activity.

### City-Level Insights

* São Paulo city emerged as the strongest individual market in terms of revenue generation.
* Rio de Janeiro and Belo Horizonte also contributed significantly to overall sales performance.

### Growth Opportunities

* Several states exhibit relatively low customer penetration and revenue contribution.
* Expanding marketing and customer acquisition efforts in underrepresented regions could reduce geographic concentration risk.

---

## Product Analysis

### Category Performance

* Beauty & Health emerged as the highest revenue-generating product category.
* Home & Lifestyle-related categories also demonstrated strong sales performance.

### Revenue Concentration

* Revenue is concentrated among a limited number of product categories.
* The top-performing categories contribute a substantial share of total business revenue.

### Freight Cost Analysis

* Categories with higher revenue often incur higher freight costs.
* Monitoring freight expenses is important to protect profitability in high-volume categories.

### Product Portfolio Optimization

* Resources should be prioritized toward top-performing categories with strong revenue contribution.
* Underperforming categories should be reviewed for pricing, promotion, or inventory optimization opportunities.

---

## Strategic Recommendations

1. Increase customer acquisition efforts in underrepresented states to reduce revenue concentration risk.
2. Continue investing in top-performing product categories while monitoring freight expenses.
3. Optimize logistics operations to maintain the current high delivery success rate.
4. Strengthen customer retention strategies to increase repeat purchases and customer lifetime value.
5. Leverage the popularity of credit card payments by introducing targeted payment-based promotions.
6. Perform deeper category-level profitability analysis to identify high-margin growth opportunities.

---

## Conclusion

The analysis reveals a healthy e-commerce business with strong revenue generation, high delivery success rates, and a large customer base. While revenue performance is strong, growth opportunities exist through geographic expansion, customer retention initiatives, and optimization of product category performance. Strategic focus on these areas can help drive sustainable long-term growth.
